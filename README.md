# Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 at IEM

Ten years ago, IBM put a quantum processor on the cloud and turned what used to be a physics-lab exclusivity into something anyone with a laptop could touch. This November, we brought that decade of progress home to Kolkata.

Welcome to the official web portal for **Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 at the Institute of Engineering and Management (IEM), Kolkata**—hosted in collaboration with **IBM Quantum**, the **IEM Centre of Excellence in Quantum Computing**, **QuantCom**, and **UEM**.

**This site has been developed by Asmita Ghorai.**

---

## The Story Behind the Fest

Our 2026 theme, **Chi(r)ps: A Decade of Harmony on the Cloud**, takes its cue from the birds IBM names its quantum processors after—and the quiet precision engineers use to tune microwave pulses until noisy qubits sing in coherence. 

Across three days (**November 9–11, 2026**), we bridged that gap between physical hardware and open-source code, bringing together curious beginners, researchers, and builders for an in-person kickoff at IEM Kolkata followed by two days of virtual deep dives and late-night hacking.

## What Happened on the Ground & in the Cloud

* **Day 1 — In-Person Kickoff at IEM Kolkata (Nov 9):** Starting with a 10:00 AM reporting call, participants dove straight into hands-on Qiskit 101 with **Dr. Kavitha Yogaraj**, circuit transpilation and noise-aware optimization with **Dr. Ritajit Majumdar**, and Quantum Machine Learning with **Amitava Chakraborty**—capping off the afternoon with high-speed quantum trivia and physical QFF26 swag.
* **Day 2 — Bridging Physical & Logical Layers (Nov 10):** Moving online, **Dr. Subrata Chakraborty** unpacked what actually happens beneath a gate—from transmon superconducting physics and system–bath decoherence to pulse-level control and processor coupling topologies.
* **The Atomiq Hackathon (Nov 10–11):** A 24-hour virtual sprint on [Devpost](https://atomiq-hackathon-qff-26-iem.devpost.com) where solo hackers and crews of up to three tackled a dedicated **Fintech** problem statement—contributed and judged by **Dr. Aniruddha Biswas**—competing for a cash prize pool, Certifier credentials, and community spotlighting.
